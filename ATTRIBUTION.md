## Board

The ChArUco board (9 × 6 squares, 30 mm squares, 22 mm markers, `DICT_5X5_100`) is generated by
OpenCV's `CharucoBoard.generateImage`; the screen and print images served at `/api/board.png` are
part of this project (MIT).
