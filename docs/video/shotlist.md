# NextView demo video: shot list

Published at https://youtu.be/0aQBOavDezg (unlisted). Master file `nextview-demo.mp4` (not committed): 1920x1080, 30 fps, 4:22.8 (262.8 s), H.264 + AAC, loudness normalised to -14 LUFS. Captions are burned in and also provided as `nextview-demo.srt`.

Screen footage is one continuous headless-browser recording of the **live endpoint** (https://t4zn85l054.execute-api.ap-southeast-1.amazonaws.com) on 23 October 2026, build `36da4a9` (pre-release, not in the public history). Speed-ups are marked on screen. The simulator tab uses a simulated camera (preset `wide_robot_handheld`, human actuator, seed 42), so its "true" errors are simulator ground truth, not real-camera results. The webcam tab was driven by a virtual camera device that plays a rendered video of the screen board (no physical camera); this is stated on screen. The single-session race chart is not evidence that the agent beats random poses: it is one seed, and the baseline keeps the k5 model, so part of its gap is the approved model switch. Policy comparisons come from the pre-registered benchmark (`docs/preregistration.md`, `results/`). The Evidence-tab shot is a full-page screenshot of the same endpoint after a redeploy (build `36da4a9-20261023063439`, pre-release, not in the public history; 2026-10-23), labelled on screen.

Results cards show the committed figures in `results/figures/` (E1, E2, E4, E5, E7, E12); the millimetre table is drawn from `results/synth_test/consequence.json`, the MCP card from `results/llm_row/` (call log `results/llm_row/qwen2.5_7b/run_1003.jsonl`). The architecture shot is `docs/architecture.svg`.

Narration: a synthetic text-to-speech voice. No face, no human voice, no music.
